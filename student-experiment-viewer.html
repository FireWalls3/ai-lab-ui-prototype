Theory & Background

Ohm's Law states that the current through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the voltage across the two points. Mathematically, V = I × R, where V is voltage (volts), I is current (amperes), and R is resistance (ohms).

Resistivity (ρ) is an intrinsic property of a material that describes how strongly it resists electrical current. It is related to resistance by R = ρL/A, where L is the length and A is the cross-sectional area of the conductor.
